WHO WE ARE & WHAT WE DO

Empowering Communities. Funding Dreams.

The Dynasty: House of Providence (Dynasty Wealth Systems) is a Christ-centered nonprofit organization dedicated to economic restoration and community revitalization. Serving as the hands and feet of Jesus, we remove financial barriers by providing free business education, mentorship, and direct grant and zero-interest micro-loan funding to help families and entrepreneurs launch and grow sustainable businesses.

OUR CORE PILLARS

Three Ways We Serve

FREE EDUCATION Zero-cost courses in business setup, budgeting, credit restoration, and financial stewardship.
HANDS-ON MENTORSHIP 1-on-1 coaching to help founders register entities, build budgets, and earn certification.
GRANTS & 0% CAPITAL Direct Launch Grants and zero-interest micro-loans to fund verified startup expenses.
EDUCATION BEFORE ELEVATION

How It Works

A transparent, self-sustaining cycle designed to equip founders and recycle capital back into the community.

STEP 01

Enroll & Learn Free

Join at zero cost to access foundational business, credit, and stewardship training.

STEP 02

Build Your Blueprint

Partner with a DWS coach to form your legal entity, build a budget, and earn certification.

STEP 03

Receive Seed Capital

Unlock $5,000 Launch Grants / 0% Seed Micro-Loans and $10,000 Growth Funding.

STEP 04

Recycle & Multiply

Repaid 0% micro-loan principal recycles directly back into the fund to bless the next founder.

How Our Programs Are Funded: Supported by philanthropic donations, foundation grants, corporate sponsorships, earned B2B educational licensing, and our self-replenishing 0% micro-loan pool.

The 12 Community & Wealth Sectors

Select a pillar below to see how we support every area of business, financial health, and community life.

Financial Literacy & Capital Access

1. Community Banking & Capital: Safe wealth storage guidance and zero-barrier startup funding.
2. Financial Education & Protection: Budgeting, income protection, and retirement readiness.
3. Capital Stewardship & Endowment: Responsible management of donor reserves to sustain grants.
4. Credit Restoration & Advocacy: Free credit education, debt reduction, and consumer advocacy.
5. Tax Compliance & Structuring: Legal entity setup, compliance, and sound accounting habits.

Business & Operational Support

6. Systems & Automation: Modern software tools and workflows for small business efficiency.
7. Marketing, Media & Branding: Helping local entrepreneurs build professional brands and reach customers.
8. Real Estate & Development: Revitalizing neighborhoods and creating affordable commercial spaces.
9. Governance & Strategic Planning: Strict legal compliance, independent board oversight, and transparency.

Youth, Wellness & Cultural Restoration

10. Youth Legacy & Education: Financial literacy and entrepreneurship mentorship for the next generation.
11. Creative Arts & Media: Uplifting arts, music, and media initiatives that strengthen culture.
12. Holistic Wellness & Restoration: Supporting physical, mental, emotional, and spiritual health.
