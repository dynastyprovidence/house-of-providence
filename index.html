WHO WE ARE & WHAT WE DO

Empowering Communities. Funding Dreams.

The Dynasty: House of Providence (Dynasty Wealth Systems) is a Christ-centered nonprofit organization dedicated to economic restoration and community revitalization. Serving as the hands and feet of Jesus, we remove financial barriers by providing free business education, mentorship, and direct grant and zero-interest micro-loan funding to help families and entrepreneurs launch, stabilize, and grow thriving businesses.

OUR MISSION To build wealth and rebuild communities through Christ-centered entrepreneurship, education, and direct financial empowerment.
OUR VISION To create thriving local ecosystems where families are restored, poverty is eliminated, and entrepreneurs build lasting legacies.
HOW IT WORKS — EDUCATION BEFORE ELEVATION

From Vision to Funded Business

1. Enroll & Learn Free Join at zero cost to access foundational courses, business planning templates, and credit-readiness workshops.
2. Build Your Blueprint Work alongside a dedicated DWS Coach to register your legal entity, establish a budget, and earn certification.
3. Receive Seed Capital Qualify for $5,000 Launch Grants / 0% Micro-Loans for startup costs and $10,000 Growth Funding to scale.
4. Pay It Forward Funded founders mentor incoming entrepreneurs while repaid 0% micro-loan principal recycles back into the fund.
360-DEGREE RESTORATION

The 12 Community & Wealth Sectors

A holistic ecosystem supporting every pillar of financial health, business growth, and community renewal.

Financial Literacy & Capital Access

1. Community Banking & Capital Access: Safe wealth storage guidance, financial stewardship, and zero-barrier startup funding.
2. Financial Education & Protection: Teaching budgeting, income protection, and retirement readiness.
3. Capital Stewardship & Endowment: Responsibly managing donor contributions and reserves to sustain community grants.
4. Credit Restoration & Consumer Advocacy: Free credit education, debt-reduction strategies, and consumer advocacy.
5. Tax Compliance & Business Structuring: Helping founders legally register entities, stay compliant, and build sound accounting habits.

Business & Operational Support

6. Systems & Automation: Modern software tools and automated workflows that help small businesses run efficiently.
7. Marketing, Media & Branding: Helping local entrepreneurs build professional brands and reach new customers.
8. Real Estate & Community Development: Acquiring and developing property to revitalize neighborhoods and create affordable commercial spaces.
9. Governance, Policy & Strategic Planning: Maintaining strict legal compliance, independent board oversight, and transparent leadership.

Youth, Wellness & Cultural Restoration

10. Youth Legacy & Education: Delivering financial literacy and entrepreneurship mentorship to the next generation.
11. Creative Arts & Media: Supporting uplifting arts, music, and media initiatives that strengthen community culture.
12. Holistic Wellness & Restoration: Supporting physical, mental, emotional, and spiritual health alongside financial growth.
